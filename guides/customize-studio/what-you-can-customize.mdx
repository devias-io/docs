The studio is the app a team works in. Most of what it shows comes from modules and from data, and the app itself is configured in one small file, `apps/studio/src/studio.config.tsx`, with `defineStudio`.

```tsx
export const studioConfig = defineStudio({
  title: 'Acme',
  modules,
  structure: (S) => S.list().items([S.navItems()]),
});
```

`defineStudio` takes a handful of settings:

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `title` | What the studio is called, for example on the sign-in pages. |
| `modules` | The modules to load, in order. They come from `devias.config.ts`. A later module wins when two contribute the same thing. |
| `structure` | The sidebar. See [Sidebar structure](/guides/customize-studio/structure). |
| `defaultPage` | Where people land after signing in. |
| `contact` | A link in the sidebar for reaching you, such as an email address. |
| `agentImages` | Pictures for agents, by icon name. |

## Parts that can be added

- [Sidebar structure](/guides/customize-studio/structure): what appears in the sidebar, and in what order.
- [Field and view types](/guides/customize-studio/field-and-view-types): new kinds of field and new ways to look at records.
- [Blocks](/guides/customize-studio/blocks): pieces for Home, record pages and the side panel.
- [Pages and commands](/guides/customize-studio/pages-commands): whole screens and palette commands.
- [Theming](/guides/customize-studio/theming): colours, fonts and corners.

## Where the code lives

A part can come from the app itself, in `studio.config.tsx`, or from a module's `./studio` export. A part that belongs with a module's data should live in the module, so that it travels with it.

To work on the studio without a server, run `VITE_MOCK=1 pnpm dev:studio`. It starts the studio on an in-memory fake backend.
