A **document** is a page of rich text for things that do not fit in a field, such as meeting notes, proposals and processes. **Documents** in the sidebar lists them as a gallery, most recently edited first, with a search box at the top.

## Create and write a document

1. Choose **New document**.
2. Type as in any editor. Press `/` to open the slash menu of blocks, such as headings, lists and tables.
3. Type `@` followed by a few letters to mention a record, another document or a task. The mention becomes a link to it.

## Comments

A document has a **Comments** section. A comment can be attached to the whole page or to a passage of text. A comment can be marked resolved when it is dealt with.

## Timeline

A document keeps a timeline of when it was written, who edited it, and the comments added to it.

## Where documents show up

- A task or a record can link to a document.
- A record's page lists the documents and tasks that mention it.
- An agent can read and write documents when its tools allow it.
